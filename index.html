🏏 HOW TO PLAY

🏏 BATTING (when your team bats)

1. Watch the bowler run up — the ball is coming!
2. A green timing bar slides left-right at the top. A moving white marker bounces back and forth.
3. Hit a shot button when the white marker is inside the green zone for perfect timing → more runs, fewer wickets.
4. Hit it outside the green zone → mistimed → risk of wicket or dot ball.

DEFEND 🛡️ — Safe block. Low risk, 0–1 runs. Good against any delivery.
DRIVE 🏏 — Straight/covers. Best on full-length balls. Risky on short balls.
CUT ✂️ — Point/third man. Best on short balls outside off stump.
LOFT 🚀 — Aerial shot. High risk, can score 4 or 6. Risky on yorkers.

🎳 BOWLING (when you bowl)

1. Pick your bowler from the sidebar at the start of each over.
2. Click on the pitch to aim where you want the ball to land — a target marker appears!
3. Then choose your delivery type from the buttons.

PACE BOWLERS get: Fast · Bouncer · Yorker · Inswing · Outswing
SPIN BOWLERS get: Off-break · Leg-spin · Googly · Doosra · Flipper

Aim for the stumps with a Yorker = high wicket chance.
Aim outside off stump with a bouncer = force the cut shot risk.
A Googly on a wrong-foot batter = spin surprise!
